How it works

From raw alert to a decision you can defend.

Onboarding is a connection, not a migration. Your tools, your data and your rules stay where they are.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Scoped, least-privilege API access to your detection platforms. Read-only by default.

  2. 02

    Correlate

    Alerts from different tools are normalised and joined into a single case per incident.

  3. 03

    Investigate

    Agents gather context, test hypotheses and rule out benign explanations.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Each case gets a verdict with the evidence behind it. Material cases are escalated.

  5. 05

    Respond

    Containment actions run through your tools, under the approval policy you set.

Every verdict shows its work.

A verdict without evidence just moves the trust problem. SGI cases record each step the agent took, the data it looked at, and why it reached its conclusion, so an analyst can verify it in a minute rather than redo the investigation.

  • Full investigation trail on every case
  • Benign closures are explained, not just closed
  • Analyst feedback tunes future triage
  • Reporting you can take to audit and the board

DetectionUnfamiliar sign-in location, followed by a new inbox forwarding rule

    Joined 3 alerts across identity, email and endpoint into one case.

  2. enrich

    User is a finance approver. Sign-in network not seen for this user in 90 days.

  4. verdict

    Material. Consistent with business email compromise.

  5. action

    Revoke sessions, remove rule, reset credentials. Awaiting analyst approval

before launch

Pre-launch assessment

A point-in-time review before a product, feature or market launch. You get a gap analysis against each applicable law and a remediation plan ranked by risk.

ongoing

Continuous compliance

Agents re-check your live sites and evidence on a schedule, so the auditor's pack is current when they ask for it, not rebuilt the week before.

What we assess

  • Data inventory and mappingWhat personal data you collect, where it flows and who can reach it.
  • Lawful basis, notice and consentNotices, consent capture and records, including cookie consent.
  • Individual rightsAccess, correction, deletion and opt-out requests, end to end.
  • Processors and transfersVendor contracts and cross-border data transfers.
  • Safeguards and breach readinessSecurity controls and the notification plan regulators expect.
  • Retention and deletionHow long data is kept, and proof that it is removed.

What agents find on live sites

  • Trackers before consentAnalytics or ad tags that fire before the visitor chooses.
  • Ignored opt-outsRejecting cookies or Global Privacy Control signals with no effect.
  • Personal data in URLs and logsEmails or IDs leaking through query strings and referrers.
  • Broken rights requestsRequest forms that fail, stall or never reach a person.
  • Notice driftPrivacy notices that no longer match what the site actually does.

Assessments support your legal counsel and auditors. They are not legal advice.
